## 🚨 Legal Consequences

### Civil Penalties:

Unauthorized use may result in:

- **Statutory damages**: $750 to $150,000 per work infringed (17 U.S.C. § 504)
- **Actual damages** and **profits** of the infringer
- **Attorney's fees** and **legal costs** (often exceeding $100,000)
- **Injunctive relief** (court order to stop use)
- **Seizure** of infringing materials
- **Destruction** of unauthorized copies

### Criminal Penalties:

Willful copyright infringement for commercial advantage may result in:

- **Up to 5 years imprisonment** (first offense)
- **Up to 10 years imprisonment** (subsequent offenses)
- **Fines up to $250,000** for individuals
- **Fines up to $500,000** for organizations
- **Criminal record**

### Trade Secret Violations:

Under the Economic Espionage Act:

- **Up to 10 years imprisonment**
- **Fines up to $5,000,000** for organizations
- **Criminal forfeiture** of property
- **Restitution** to the victim company

### Immediate Actions:

2. **Documentation**: We collect evidence of infringement
